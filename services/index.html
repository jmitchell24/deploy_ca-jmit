Bench Service

This is my base hourly rate for any work done in my workshop. This includes diagnostics, troubleshooting, repair, and configuration.

Remote & In-Home Support

All common and routine Home IT support services are available. This includes but is not limited to:

  • New computer setup
  • Fixing Printer misconfiguration
  • Fixing Router / Network misconfiguration

If you have an issue that isn’t listed please feel free to reach out. My knowledge is broad and it is likely that I can create a solution for you.

Malware Remediation

A complete system diagnostic scan, followed by removal of all detected malware. If you have been the victim of an online or phone scam then this service can help you.

Custom PC Assembly

An end-to-end custom PC assembly service, offered at a flat-rate fee. I provide advice on part selection given your requirements and budget, and perform full assembly of your new machine, as well as initial software setup.

I am very detail oriented, and strive to ensure that my customers are fully satisfied e.g. ensuring good cable management and RGB lighting effect installation.

Website Development

Complete websites for small businesses, trades, and community organizations. If you need somewhere for customers to find your hours, your services, and how to reach you, this is that.

I offer two flat-rate tiers:

  • Single page, $600. One page covering who you are, what you offer, and how to get in touch.
  • Multiple pages, starting at $1200. Separate pages for services, contact, hours, a photo gallery, or anything else you need.

Every build includes design and development of the site, registration of your domain name (or transfer of one you already own), hosting setup, and launch.

Not included are online stores, payment processing, and ongoing search engine optimization. If you need any of these, reach out and we can talk about it.

My site designs are fast and static. There are no logins, plugins, or databases that would otherwise turn into a future security risk. The site you are reading right now is built the same way.

Turnaround on a single page site is typically two to four weeks, depending on how quickly you can get your content to me.

You own your domain and your content. If you ever decide to take the site elsewhere, both go with you.

Website Maintenance

Once your site is live you can keep it on a monthly plan for $40 CAD. This is optional, and it is not a requirement for having me build your site.

The plan covers:

  • Hosting fees and domain renewal
  • Software updates and backups
  • Uptime monitoring
  • Up to one hour of content changes each month, for things like new hours, new photos, or updated prices

Any work beyond that hour is billed at my bench rate of $50 CAD hourly. You can cancel the plan at any time.
