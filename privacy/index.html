My Privacy Policy

This policy covers how I handle your information when you use my services or contact me through jmit.ca.

What I Collect

  • Contact Details. You provide your name, email, phone number if you choose to share it, and details about your issue. This comes through the service request form or by Email, Discord, etc.
  • Remote Sessions. During remote support, I can see your screen. I do not record sessions, retain screenshots, or store any of your personal files.
  • Payments. e-Transfer payments are processed through Interac. I do not see or store your banking details.
  • Site Analytics. I do collect visitor statistics for the jmit.ca website through my own infrastructure (no third-parties such as Google). I also store browser data for functional purposes of the jmit.ca website.

What I Don’t Collect

  • I do not sell, share, or give your information to anyone.
  • I do not store passwords or credentials you share during a support session.

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used to respond to your inquiry, provide the service you’ve requested, and send invoices or follow up on completed work. Nothing else.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Forms. The service request form is hosted by Google. Their privacy policy applies to form submissions.
  • RustDesk. Remote support sessions use RustDesk, connected through a server that I operate. Session data passes through my server only during the active session and is not stored.

Data Retention

I keep your contact information and service history for bookkeeping and follow-up. If you want me to delete your information, I will do so. Just contact me directly with your request.
