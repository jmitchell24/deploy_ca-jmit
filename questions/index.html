Common Questions

What is Remote Support?

I connect to your computer using secure remote access software. You can see everything I’m doing on your screen. Most issues can be resolved this way. It means we both get to stay home. 😉

Securty during Remote Sessions

I use industry-standard remote access tools. You can watch everything happening on screen, and you can disconnect the session at any time. I do not store any of your personal data.

What if your problem can’t be fixed remotely?

I offer in-home support in Haldimand and Norfolk County.

What payments do I accept?

I accept cash payment, as well as Interac e-Transfer.

Do I work on Android devices, Macs or other Apple products?

My focus is Windows and Linux systems. I have limited experience repairing and supporting Apple/Android devices. If you have an Apple/Android related question, feel free to reach out. I may still be able to help.

Do you sell computer parts?

For custom builds, I help you choose the right parts for your budget and build the system for you. You purchase the parts directly (I can send you the links), or I can order them on your behalf.

What are my hours of operation?

I am available for work 9:00am - 5:00pm, every day of the week. I respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours.

See the about page for more details.

Do you provide any pro-bono (free) services?

For the initial launch of jmit.ca, I am offering pro-bono services on a case-by-case basis.

I will choose at my sole discretion what services to provide, and to whom. I will honour completely any agreements that I make for service, but I reserve the right to stop accepting new pro-bono work at any time.

Paid service must be prioritised to sustain the business, but otherwise I will maintain the same high standard of service and timeliness that I provide to paying customers.

I am specifically making this offer for registered non-profit and community organisations. If you represent such an organisation I encourage you to reach out. See the about page for more details.
